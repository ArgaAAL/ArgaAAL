### 🔭 Research Focus: The Intersection of AI & Society

> *"We shape our tools, and thereafter our tools shape us."*

Beyond code, I analyze the **socioeconomic impact** of algorithmic systems and the eventual **commoditization of labor**.

1.  **AI Alignment & Bias:**
    * Investigating how LLMs "parrot" training data biases (Western/Academic skew).
    * Implementing **RLHF** (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback) strategies for cleaner inference.
2.  **Forensic Truth:**
    * Exploring **Zero-Knowledge (zkML)** proofs to verify model weights and copyright compliance without exposing proprietary data.
3.  **Post-Labor Economics:**
    * Designing architectures that anticipate the automation of repetitive software development tasks by generative AI.
